# NECA Lab. · Website design preview

This is the bilingual review site at [GitHub Pages](https://ohseongwoog.github.io/necalab/preview/). The [existing lab website](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/home) remains separate.

The home page leads with public lab news. Its research section links to five areas: Radar & Physical AI, AI for RF Design, 3D Chip Interconnects, RF for Fusion Plasmas, and Evidence-Grounded AI. Each area has an original, editable SVG concept diagram. The two groups in Recent research outputs are peer-reviewed journal articles and confirmed international conference presentations. They use separate inclusion checks; news items do not appear as publications.

The public catalog contains 23 journals, nine international conference contributions, seven people and four lab announcements. Source records and links stay with the entries. The lab’s [news](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/news), [publications](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/Journal), [faculty](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/professor) and [members](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/members) pages are the main public references. The illustrations are explanatory, not measured data. The interactive home-page polar graphic is a calculated array factor for a uniform square array.

The original lab logo and seven public portraits are preserved; the optional EM/AI logo study remains separate. Institutional colors follow the [University of Seoul visual identity](https://www.uos.ac.kr/eng/web/contents/UOS_About_04_02_03). The university emblem is not redrawn.

To rebuild, use Python 3 in this directory: `python3 build.py`, then `python3 validate.py`. The six HTML pages, their relative assets and bilingual catalog do not require a server-side application. The draft is public, with noindex metadata. No private project records are distributed.
