# NECA Lab. · Website design preview

This is the bilingual review site at [GitHub Pages](https://ohseongwoog.github.io/necalab/preview/). The [existing lab website](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/home) remains separate.

Public lab news is the first section below the compact hero. Its research section links to five areas: Radar & Physical AI, AI for RF Design, 3D Chip Interconnects, RF for Fusion Plasmas, and Evidence-Grounded AI. Each area has an original, editable SVG concept diagram. The two groups in Recent research outputs are peer-reviewed journal articles and confirmed international conference presentations. They use separate inclusion checks; news items do not appear as publications.

The public catalog contains 23 journals, nine international conference contributions, seven people and four lab announcements. Source records and links stay with the entries. The lab’s [news](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/news), [publications](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/Journal), [faculty](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/professor) and [members](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/members) pages are the main public references. The illustrations are explanatory, not measured data. The hero contains two always-visible, single-slider examples: no login, text entry, start button, or external requests. In the first, visitors blend two fixed clues (a Wi-Fi drop and an 82 °C reading) in a two-class teaching model; text and sensor evidence favor different labels. It is not a trained model. In the second, moving the receiver changes the drawn RF wave and the received level in an ideal 2.45 GHz free-space link. Transmit power is −5 dBm, both antennas are isotropic, and the notional receiver's threshold is −63 dBm. Below threshold the RF wave still exists, but the link is marked disconnected. No radio hardware is controlled. The [University of Toronto’s Friis lecture](https://radiance.ece.utoronto.ca/ece422/notes/12-friiseffects.pdf) is technical background; no third-party code or runtime libraries are used.

The original lab logo and seven public portraits are preserved; the optional EM/AI logo study remains separate. Institutional colors follow the [University of Seoul visual identity](https://www.uos.ac.kr/eng/web/contents/UOS_About_04_02_03). The university emblem is not redrawn.

To rebuild, use Python 3 in this directory: `python3 build.py`, then `python3 validate.py`. The six HTML pages, their relative assets and bilingual catalog do not require a server-side application. The draft is public, with noindex metadata. No private project records are distributed.

## Interaction design

The Home and Research pages share a large, swipeable, English-language gallery. Each field starts with its original diagram and title. Selecting the picture or title reveals a brief explanation and a distinct visual cue. The currently selected image loops while on screen, but the carousel never advances on its own. Other cards and hidden tabs do not animate. Reduced-motion mode keeps the diagrams static. The five research fragments and the current and legacy experiment links remain addressable. Editorial references included [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/) for direct questions, [Google Research](https://research.google/research-areas/) for topic navigation, and the [W3C carousel pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/) for keyboard and motion controls.
