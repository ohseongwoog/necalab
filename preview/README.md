# NECA Lab. · Website design preview

This is the bilingual review site at [GitHub Pages](https://ohseongwoog.github.io/necalab/preview/). The [existing lab website](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/home) remains separate.

The homepage masthead pairs the lab name with a swipeable gallery of five portrait research plates (3:4); lab news follows immediately. The five research fields are Radar & Physical AI, AI for RF Design, 3D Chip Interconnects, RF for Fusion Plasmas, and Evidence-Grounded AI. Each area has an original, editable SVG concept diagram. The two groups in Recent research outputs are peer-reviewed journal articles and confirmed international conference presentations. They use separate inclusion checks; news items do not appear as publications.

The public catalog contains 23 journals, nine international conference contributions, seven people and four lab announcements. Source records and links stay with the entries. The lab’s [news](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/news), [publications](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/Journal), [faculty](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/professor) and [members](https://sites.google.com/view/necalab/members) pages are the main public references. The illustrations are explanatory, not measured data. Two short, browser-local puzzles form the last block on Home. Each has three native sliders, visible goal bands and immediate directional hints; reloading draws new targets without interrupting a session or saving activity. AI visitors calibrate three evidence channels in a fixed teaching model: text, sensor and log weights each contribute strongly to one diagnostic score and weakly to the other two (0.76/0.12/0.12). All three scores must land in their bands. This is not a trained classifier. The RF challenge tunes distance, relative linear polarization angle and the fraction of power delivered by matching. Its ideal 2.45 GHz link budget uses −5 dBm transmit power, unit antenna gains, free-space distance loss, cos²(angle) and the displayed matching efficiency. The received level must land within a target band, not simply be as strong as possible. None of the goal bands can be reached by leaving even one control at its initial value. No radio hardware, backend, tracking or model service is involved. The [University of Toronto’s Friis lecture](https://radiance.ece.utoronto.ca/ece422/notes/12-friiseffects.pdf) is technical background.

The original lab logo and seven public portraits are preserved; the optional EM/AI logo study remains separate. Institutional colors follow the [University of Seoul visual identity](https://www.uos.ac.kr/eng/web/contents/UOS_About_04_02_03). The university emblem is not redrawn.

To rebuild, use Python 3 in this directory: `python3 build.py`, then `python3 validate.py`. The six HTML pages, their relative assets and bilingual catalog do not require a server-side application. The draft is public, with noindex metadata. No private project records are distributed.

## Interaction design

Home presents a portrait composition for each field, whereas Research retains the detailed landscape plates. Both galleries are swipeable, with an English field title inside each card. Selecting the picture or title reveals a brief explanation and a distinct visual cue. The currently selected image loops while on screen, but the carousel never advances on its own. Other cards and hidden tabs do not animate. Reduced-motion mode keeps the diagrams static. The five research fragments and the current and legacy experiment links remain addressable. Editorial references included [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/) for direct questions, [Google Research](https://research.google/research-areas/) for topic navigation, and the [W3C carousel pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/) for keyboard and motion controls.
